# ExamShield — ISMS consultancy

ExamShield is a fictional digital examination and academic assessment service used to explore risk-based information security management. The consultancy design focuses on confidentiality of examination questions, integrity of academic results, and availability of examination services.

## Scope
- Business process and information asset analysis
- Requirements engineering and proposed secure architecture
- Risk assessment, treatment and residual-risk planning
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 information security management programme
- UK data protection, NIST and Cyber Essentials alignment
- Offline browser-based workflow demonstrator and discussion-based incident exercises

## Evidence status
The demonstrator uses synthetic records and simulated personas. It does not provide real authentication, encryption, a server, durable storage or a production security monitoring system. Proposed controls, risk ratings, recovery objectives and certification readiness require implementation and independent verification.

## Repository use
This public repository contains a non-sensitive project overview. Full coursework, student identifiers, university materials and participant evidence are retained separately. No claim of certification or production security is made.
